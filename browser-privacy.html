Xtaff Browser Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09 · Operated by 熙宇科技

Xtaff Browser is a Chrome extension that connects a local AI assistant to a browser through a local MCP host. This policy applies to the extension, not the separate Xtaff Gmail connector.

Data processed and why

To carry out the user's browser tasks, the extension can process tab identifiers, URLs and origins; page text, structure, form information and screenshots; and diagnostic information such as console messages, network paths or statuses, and dialogs. Pages may contain personal or sensitive information. The extension can also interact with websites and handle files when the assistant requests those actions.

The extension controls tabs created in or explicitly brought into the Xtaff tab group. By default, access to each site requires the user's approval; the user can instead enable full authorization for eligible sites in that group. Cross-origin frames are subject to the selected authorization mode.

Local processing and sharing

The extension communicates with a native host and the user's AI assistant on the same computer. It does not use a developer-operated cloud relay or analytics service. Page data and screenshots returned to the assistant may be retained or sent to an AI model provider or other service by that assistant, depending on the user's assistant and its configuration. Such processing is governed by the relevant provider's policies. Browser actions requested by the assistant can also send information to the websites the user interacts with.

We do not sell user data or use it for advertising. Xtaff Browser's use of information adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and controls

Site approvals, authorization mode, stop state, site playbooks, and pending playbook drafts are stored locally in Chrome. Playbooks contain site-specific knowledge proposed by an assistant and may be shared with connected local assistants on request. Proposals are sanitized before storage and become active only after user approval; sanitization may not remove all sensitive information. Temporary browser diagnostic records are held in memory and released when the corresponding tab is closed; certain authorization changes clear them sooner. The assistant or its provider may keep its own records under its own settings and policies.

Users can revoke approved sites, disable full authorization, stop or resume control in the extension popup, and remove a tab from the Xtaff group. Users can review, approve, delete, export, and restore playbooks through the extension options page. Removing the extension clears its local extension storage.

Sensitive information

The extension attempts to identify and redact sensitive fields, and masks recognized sensitive fields in screenshots. If screenshot masking cannot be applied, capture is refused. These protections are not comprehensive: secrets displayed as ordinary text or in unrecognized fields may still appear in page data, diagnostics, or screenshots. Review what the assistant can access before approving a site.

For privacy questions, contact qiweiview@gmail.com.

Xtaff Browser 隐私政策

更新日期：2026-11-09 · 运营方：熙宇科技

Xtaff Browser 是通过本机 MCP 宿主连接 AI 助手与 Chrome 的浏览器扩展。本政策适用于该扩展；Gmail 连接器隐私政策另行说明。

处理的数据和用途

为执行用户提出的浏览器任务，扩展可能处理标签标识、网址和来源，网页文字、结构、表单信息及截图，以及控制台消息、网络路径或状态和弹窗等诊断信息。网页可能包含个人或敏感信息。助手请求时，扩展也可以操作网页或处理文件。

扩展只控制在 Xtaff 标签组中创建或由用户明确放入该组的标签。默认逐站获取用户授权；用户也可以开启组内符合条件网站的全授权。跨域页面框架受所选授权模式约束。

本机处理与共享

扩展与本机宿主和用户的 AI 助手在同一台计算机上通信，不使用开发者运营的云中转或分析服务。扩展返回给助手的网页数据和截图，可能由助手根据自身配置保存或发送给 AI 模型提供商等服务，并受相应服务的政策约束。助手执行的网页操作也可能向用户访问的网站发送信息。

我们不出售用户数据或将其用于广告。Xtaff Browser 对信息的使用遵守 Chrome Web Store 用户数据政策，包括有限使用要求。

存储与控制

站点授权、授权模式、停止状态、站点秘籍及待审草稿保存在 Chrome 本地。站点秘籍包含助手提出的站点操作经验，可应请求提供给已连接的本机助手。提案保存前会进行清理，并须经用户批准后才会生效；清理可能无法移除所有敏感信息。临时浏览器诊断记录保存在内存中，相关标签关闭时释放；某些授权变更会提前清除记录。助手或其服务商可能依据各自的设置和政策保留记录。

用户可在扩展弹窗撤销站点授权、关闭全授权、停止或恢复控制，也可以将标签移出 Xtaff 组。用户可在扩展选项页查看、批准、删除、导出和恢复站点秘籍。移除扩展会清除扩展的本地存储。

敏感信息

扩展尝试识别并脱敏敏感字段，并在截图中遮罩已识别的敏感字段；无法完成截图遮罩时将拒绝截图。这些保护并不全面：普通文本或未识别字段中的秘密仍可能出现在网页数据、诊断信息或截图中。批准站点前，请留意助手可访问的内容。
