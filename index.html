Xtaff Browser.

Xtaff Browser connects a local AI coding assistant to Chrome so it can inspect and interact with authorized web pages at your direction.

The extension and the Gmail connector are separate products. Learn about browser data, permissions, and the local assistant in the Xtaff Browser privacy policy.

Gmail, connected with purpose.

An organization can authorize a dedicated Gmail mailbox for its Xtaff agents. When asked to perform mail tasks, agents can search and read messages or send replies from the authorized mailbox.

Google access is granted through OAuth and can be revoked from your Google Account. Our privacy policy explains what data the connector accesses, how it is used, and how access can be revoked.
